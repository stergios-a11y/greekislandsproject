5 December · Sunday

AmmoulianiReligiousconfirm locally

Feast of Agios Nikolaos (patron), Ammouliani

5–6 December

The historic church of Agios Nikolaos, restored in 2022 with funds from shipowner Athanasios Martinos, is the island's parish church and its patron's feast on 5-6 December is the one winter day the fishing village fills up, with vespers, liturgy and a blessing at the harbour. The ferry from Tripiti takes ten minutes and runs all year.

AnafiPanigiriconfirm locally

Feast of Agios Nikolaos, Chora

5–6 December

Anafi's patron saint has his church on the main square of Chora and the island keeps his day, 6 December, with a liturgy and a formal communal meal organised by the cultural association. It's a residents' winter feast — a handful of visitors at most — and reaching the island in December depends on the weekly Santorini boat and the weather.

Panigiri of Agios Nikolaos, Koumbara

5–6 December

The chapel of the sailors' saint at Koumbara beach, west of the port, is where Ios celebrates 6 December: liturgy, then food and wine for the handful of islanders who are around in winter. Worth knowing if you are on Ios out of season.

Crete (Lasithi)Religiousconfirm locally

Feast of Agios Nikolaos, patron of Agios Nikolaos town

5–6 December

The town named after the saint keeps 6 December as its patronal feast: vespers on the 5th, hierarchical liturgy and a procession of the icon through the centre and along the lake on the 6th, with the municipal band and school parades, and the town's Christmas lights switched on around the same days. The tiny 8th-century chapel of Agios Nikolaos at the Minos Beach headland, which gave the town its name, also holds a liturgy.

6 December · Monday

SkiathosArts & cultureconfirm locally

Lighting of the Christmas boat, Bourtzi, Skiathos Town

6 December

Skiathos, a sailors' island, decorates boats rather than trees: on St Nicholas' day, 6 December, the municipality lights the big illuminated boat by the Bourtzi at the old port at 8 pm and switches on the town lights, with carols and a stall or two, and smaller lit boats appear in house windows. It opens a month of Christmas events at the Bourtzi cultural centre (bazaars, Karagiozis, school plays). Ferries run a reduced winter schedule from Volos and Agios Konstantinos, so check them first.

11 December · Saturday

ElafonisosReligious

Feast of Agios Spyridon, patron of Elafonisos

11–12 December · Elafonisos (harbour church)

The patron's winter feast at the church of Agios Spyridon on the harbour on 12 December: vespers on the 11th, liturgy and a procession of the icon along the quay on the 12th, and a reception offered by the parish. The island has a few hundred winter residents and one or two kafeneia open; the ferry from Pounta runs all year.

Panigiri of Agios Spyridon, Fry

11–12 December

The port town's own winter panigiri, on 11-12 December at the church of Agios Spyridon in Fry, with vespers and a glendi indoors with lyra, sweets and wine. One of the few times to see Kasos with only Kasiots on it.

16 December · Thursday

ZakynthosReligious

Feast of Agios Dionysios (his dormition), Zakynthos town

16–18 December

The winter feast of the patron: after the liturgy on 17 December the incorrupt body is carried through the town to Solomos Square and back via Agios Markos with the philharmonics of Zakynthos, Artemision and Alykes, the schools and a dozen visiting bishops; the body stays out for veneration for two more days before being returned to its silver casket. Cold, crowded and very Zakynthian; the pastry shops around Agios Markos do the best mandolato.
