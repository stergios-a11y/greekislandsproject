Crete (Heraklion)Music

Matala Beach Festival

a weekend in late June or early July, 3 days

Free three-day music festival on the beach and in the village since 2011, with stages in front of the hippie caves, on the square and by the bridge; Greek pop-rock headliners, tribute bands and DJ parties, plus street-painting, yoga and kids' activities. Dates move: 4–6 July 2025, 19–21 June 2026. The village is overwhelmed, so stay in Pitsidia or Kalamaki, or book Matala Camping months ahead.

Kimolos International Film Festival (KIFF), Chorio

late June, about 5 days

A small open-air short-film festival started by the volunteer group 'Kimolistes' and now backed by the municipality, screened at the outdoor cinema Kalisperitis in Chorio over roughly five nights in late June (25–30 June in recent listings). Fishermen's Festival (Giorti tou Psara)

last Saturday of June · Pano Koufonisi (Chora)

On the last Saturday of June the fishermen of Koufonisi, one of the biggest fishing fleets per head in Greece, cook their catch on the harbour square and hand out fresh fish, fish soup, meze and drinks free, with traditional music and dancing; the shops close for the night. Get there by 21:00 and bring cash for the tavernas afterwards.

PsaraArts & culture

Commemoration of the Psara Holocaust

Last Sunday of June

Tiny Psara remembers the 1824 Ottoman destruction that wiped out one of the revolution's great naval islands — the last defenders blew up their own powder magazine at Mavri Rachi rather than surrender. The last Sunday of June brings a memorial service, wreaths and cultural events. Solomos's poem about the 'black ridge of Psara' is part of every Greek's schooling.

SamosPanigiri

Klidonas of Agios Ioannis, Mytilinioi

Saturday nearest 24 June (21 June 2025)

The cultural association of Mytilinioi revives the Klidonas custom in the village square on the Saturday around St John's day: 'silent water', fortune-telling rhymes, burning the May Day wreaths, a traditional orchestra and a tsampouna (bagpipe) dance teacher, from 19:30. Tables are reserved in advance, so call the association. The same association runs the big Exaltation of the Cross panigiri here on 13 September (evening) with the village's own musicians.

Mamma Mia! summer (informal)

Throughout summer (June-September)

Not an official festival but a phenomenon: Skopelos hosts a steady stream of Mamma Mia! pilgrimages to Agios Ioannis chapel (the wedding scene). Local tavernas screen the films, host themed nights, and run weekly tribute events through summer. The tourism economy runs on it.

20–21 June · Chora (Messaria)

One of the two feasts Kythnos itself names as its most important: Agia Triada in Chora, on the eve and day of Agiou Pnevmatos (Whit Monday). Klidonas bonfires of Ai-Giannis, Skiathos Town

23–24 June

On the evening of 23 June the old neighbourhoods of the Chora (Plakes, Agios Nikolaos, around the church of Ai-Giannis) light bonfires, burn the May Day wreaths and children jump the flames three times for luck. It is a neighbourhood custom, not a ticketed event, so just walk up from the old port after 9 pm and follow the smoke.

Vigil of Agios Ioannis Prodromos, Kampos (Skopelos)

23–24 June · Kampos (Skopelos Town)

The small women's monastery of Prodromos a few km from the Chora keeps an all-night vigil on the eve of 24 June and offers food to those who stay, in the Athonite manner the island's monasteries follow. Klidonas

24 June

Folk Saint John's Eve tradition still alive on Milos: at sunset, unmarried girls collect water from a well in silence and predict their future husbands through tokens placed in it overnight. Bonfires are jumped on the 24th. Smaller and more intimate than the better-known Cretan version.
