Crete (Heraklion)Music

Matala Beach Festival

a weekend in late June or early July, 3 days

Free three-day music festival on the beach and in the village since 2011, with stages in front of the hippie caves, on the square and by the bridge; Greek pop-rock headliners, tribute bands and DJ parties, plus street-painting, yoga and kids' activities. Dates move: 4–6 July 2025, 19–21 June 2026. The village is overwhelmed, so stay in Pitsidia or Kalamaki, or book Matala Camping months ahead.

Panigiri of Agioi Anargyroi, Skala

30 June – 1 July

The blue-domed church above Skala beach is Agioi Anargyroi, and its feast on 1 July (vespers on the 30th) is the main panigiri of the port village, with food and music by the church after the service. Agistri is 20 minutes from Aegina by boat, so it is easy to combine with a day on the beach.

AmorgosPanigiri

Panigiri of Agioi Anargyroi, Tholaria

30 June – 1 July

Tholaria above Aegiali celebrates its parish saints on 30 June-1 July: great vespers on the eve, liturgy and procession of the icons on the day, and both evenings the whole village eats patatato (goat with potatoes) and xydato (sour meat soup) in the square and dances to violin and laouto. Vegetarian dishes replace the meat if the feast falls on a fasting day. Stay in Aegiali and walk or take the shuttle up.

Panigiri of Agioi Anargyroi, Karavostamo

30 June – 1 July

Karavostamo, the big seaside village between Evdilos and Agios Kirykos, opens its summer with Agioi Anargyroi on the evening of 30 June; the same village does Agia Paraskevi (25 July) and the huge 15 August feast, so it is the busiest panigiri village on the north coast. Feast of Panagia Therapevousa, Agios Nikolaos, Selinia

30 June – 1 July

The seaside parish of Agios Nikolaos in Selinia, on the east coast facing Piraeus, keeps the feast of its icon of Panagia Therapevousa (the Healer) on 30 June-1 July: vespers with artoklasia at 7 pm on the eve, liturgy at 7 am and a procession of the icon along the waterfront. Alonissos Traditional Dance Festival, Patitiri

first days of July, 3 days

Dance groups from all over Greece and abroad take over the island for three days at the start of July (1-3 July in 2023, 3-5 July 2026 for the 10th edition), with parades through Patitiri, open rehearsals, evening performances and a closing glenti with local food. Annual cultural festival named after the island's most famous son. Open-air concerts, theatre, the symbolic re-reading of the Hippocratic Oath at the Asklepieion archaeological site. Major draws are the dance performances and ancient drama productions in atmospheric settings.

Crete (Lasithi)Panigiri

Kornaria Festival (Sitia)

Early July through mid-August

Eastern Crete's big summer festival, named after the 17th-century Sitia-born poet Vitsentzos Kornaros, author of 'Erotokritos'. Six weeks of concerts, dance, theatre and exhibitions in and around the Venetian Kazarma fortress, plus village panigiria with raki, Cretan bands and communal tables. One of the most honest cultural experiences on the island.

a Saturday evening in the second half of July

Since 1984 the Apollon society of Karya has thrown a summer evening in the plane-tree square devoted to riganada, the Lefkadian peasant snack of dry bread soaked in water and doused with oregano, olive oil, vinegar and coarse salt, served with olives, salted sardines and local wine, then a live orchestra and dancing. Running since 1987, Rethymno turns its Venetian past into a summer-long stage. Theatre, music and dance — Cretan Renaissance works alongside Shakespeare and Molière — staged mostly at the open-air Erofili theatre inside the Fortezza, the great fortress above the old town. SkiathosReligious

Feast of the Finding of the Icon of Panagia Eikonistria, Skiathos Town

4–5 July

Skiathos' second feast of its patroness marks the finding of the icon in a pine tree in 1655. On the evening of 4 July the icon is carried from Treis Ierarches to the church of Genniseos tis Theotokou for vespers with several bishops, and on the morning of the 5th, after the liturgy, it is processed through the streets of the Chora. Pilgrims come from the mainland, so the town churches are packed; it is a religious event with no glenti attached.

9–12 July

Elafonisos celebrates its union with Greece (6 July 1850) and the consecration of its harbour church together: bells and flags on the 6th, and on the 9th a hierarchical liturgy with the Metropolitan of Monemvasia and Sparta followed by a procession of the icon of Agios Spyridon around the harbour with children in costume, then cultural performances on the harbour square in the evening. Panigiri of Agios Soulas (Silas), Soroni

29–31 July

The biggest country fair on Rhodes: a two-week run of events in the pine woods around the Agios Soulas hermitage above Soroni, peaking on 28-30 July with donkey and horse races on the little hippodrome, a contest for the best traditional reed hut (kalyva), souvlaki stalls and dancing to local bands. People come from every village on the island, so arrive before sunset on the 29th to park anywhere near the site.

Night procession of the Panagia icon from the monastery

31 July · Psara

At midnight on 31 July the young people of Psara walk three hours up to the monastery of the Dormition in the north of the island and carry the icon back down to the village at dawn, where it stays until the feast. A moving, entirely local custom; join the walk with a torch and good shoes, or wait at the harbour for the arrival.
