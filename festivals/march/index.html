Skyrian Carnival (Apokries)

13–14 March

Among the most distinctive carnivals in Greece — the 'Yeros' figure dressed in goat skins and bells dances through the village. Pre-Christian roots, deeply Skyrian.

14 March · Sunday

ChiosCarnival

Mostra of Thymiana (carnival)

14–16 March

Chios's famous carnival custom, listed in the national intangible-heritage register: on Carnival Sunday (the last Sunday before Clean Monday) Thymiana, just south of Chios town, re-enacts the villagers' victory over pirates with the 'talimi' sword dance and a procession of masked 'Mostres', now combined with a float parade. Run by the MEOTH association over the last carnival weekend, with thousands attending including visitors from Turkey. Go early, the village roads are closed.

HydraCarnivalconfirm locally

Hydra Carnival parade to Kamini

14 March · Hydra Town / Kamini

On the last Sunday of Apokries the islanders parade in fancy dress from the harbour along the coast path to Kamini, with music, a giant papier-mâché head and horsemen (and donkeys, this being Hydra), ending at the tavernas of Kamini. Tsiknopempti before it means street barbecues on the port. Ferries from Piraeus run all winter, and rooms are at their cheapest.

PsaraCarnivalconfirm locally

Carnival 'boules' and the 'alani', Psara

14 March

Through the weekends of the Triodion and on Tsiknopempti, masked 'boules' in improvised costumes go from house to house; on the final carnival Sunday the village sets tables in a circle in the big square (the 'alani'), men and women at separate tables, with ouzo, mezedes, spinach pies and hours of carnival and folk songs. Winter ferries permitting, it is the most intimate carnival in the Aegean.

Crete (Rethymno)Carnival

Rethymno Carnival (grand parade)

14 March

Crete's biggest carnival, running the whole Triodion with a treasure hunt, a night parade and a children's parade, and peaking on Cheese Sunday (the day before Clean Monday) with the grand parade along the waterfront: in 2026, 28 floats and about 11,000 participants, ending in fireworks. Hotels in the old town book out for that weekend; the parade starts mid-afternoon and the streets around the harbour close from midday.

ThasosCarnivalconfirm locally

Carnival of Panagia and Potamia

14–15 March

Thasos's carnival is concentrated in the neighbouring villages of Panagia and Potamia on the last carnival weekend: satirical floats, plenty of food and wine, dancing and the burning of the carnival effigy, then kites and Lenten food on Clean Monday. Potamia also revives the 'Anavrochiaris' rain custom at the end of August (30-31 Aug 2026).

TinosCarnivalconfirm locally

Carnival of Falatados

14 March

On the last Sunday of Carnival the big village of Falatados stages a themed parade with satirical floats, traditional instruments and a raki-fuelled street party, the largest carnival on Tinos. Nearby Agapi does the 'Makaronas' straw-effigy procession ending in a communal pasta meal, and Triantaros reads out satirical verses on Carnival Thursday.

15 March · Monday

ChiosCarnival

Clean Monday panigiri, Nenita

15 March

The mastic village of Nenita in the south ends carnival with a traditional Clean Monday panigiri in the village square, organised by the 'Vokaria' cultural association with a live folk orchestra, Lenten food and dancing. Nenita also holds a summer panigiri for Agioi Anargyroi (1 July) and an early-August village feast.

KarpathosCarnivalconfirm locally

Carnival court of Othos

15 March

On Clean Monday the village of Othos stages a mock 'court' with masqueraders (the mousoutades) trying and sentencing villagers for the year's misdeeds, followed by traditional dances. It is the best-known carnival custom on Karpathos, and the only reason many islanders come home in winter. Dress warmly; Othos is at 500 m.

LesvosCarnival

Agiasos Carnival (Agiasotiki Apokria)

15–17 March

The most literary carnival in the Aegean: the 'Satyros' carnival society of Agiasos writes satirical verse in the local dialect, performs it in the streets and runs masked parties, a children's masquerade and a 'patinada' with traditional musicians on Carnival Sunday, all culminating on Clean Monday with kites, free bean soup and rice pudding and the big satirical procession. Runs the last two weekends before Lent; the Monday is the day to be there.

Crete (Rethymno)Carnival

Clean Monday customs, Gerani and Armenoi

15 March

The Rethymno carnival closes on Clean Monday in two villages just outside town: Gerani (west, on the coast road) and Armenoi (south, on the Spili road) stage the old Cretan Lenten customs, mock weddings and satirical skits, with lagana, olives, taramas and wine for everyone and lyra players in the square. Go around midday; both are 10–15 minutes from Rethymno.

Panigiri of Panagia Evangelistria, Pigadia

24–25 March

The island capital's own feast, on Annunciation day: liturgy and procession at Evangelistria in Pigadia, then a town-wide glendi. Olympos marks the same day. A good reason to be on Karpathos out of season, and the ferries run.

SifnosPanigiriconfirm locally

Panigiri of Panagia tou Vounou, above Platys Gialos

24–25 March · Platys Gialos road

The hilltop monastery of Panagia tou Vounou, with the best view over Platys Gialos bay, celebrates the Annunciation with vespers and a Lenten panigiri on the evening of 24 March — revithada and cod rather than lamb, since it falls in Lent, plus the violin-laouto takimi. Out of season the island is quiet and this is a genuinely local night; it's on the Apollonia–Platys Gialos road, so easy to reach by car.

25 March · Thursday

Feast of the Annunciation, Messaria

25 March

The hamlet of Messaria keeps 25 March with a liturgy and kerasmata for the few dozen islanders who are around in spring, doubling as the national holiday. Only for those already on the island out of season.
