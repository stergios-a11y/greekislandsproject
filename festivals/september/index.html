SkopelosReligious

Mamma Mia! summer (informal)

Throughout summer (June-September)

Not an official festival but a phenomenon: Skopelos hosts a steady stream of Mamma Mia! pilgrimages to Agios Ioannis chapel (the wedding scene). Local tavernas screen the films, host themed nights, and run weekly tribute events through summer. The tourism economy runs on it.

Crete (Heraklion)Arts & culture

Heraklion Summer Arts Festival

1 July to late September

The municipality's summer season: over 130 concerts, plays, dance and children's shows at the Nikos Kazantzakis open-air theatre by the Venetian walls, the Manos Hatzidakis theatre and the Vethlehem bastion (1 July – 21 September in 2025), with an open-air cinema and forty-odd free events. The Festival of the Walls closes it in early September. Tickets go on sale per event on the municipality site; the Kazantzakis theatre is a ten-minute walk from Lions Square.

source
Crete (Rethymno)Music

Renaissance Festival

Late July through early September

Running since 1987, Rethymno turns its Venetian past into a summer-long stage. Theatre, music and dance — Cretan Renaissance works alongside Shakespeare and Molière — staged mostly at the open-air Erofili theatre inside the Fortezza, the great fortress above the old town. Book ahead for the headline nights.

SymiArts & culture

Symi Festival

July to September, most nights in August · Gialos / Chorio

Three-month cultural festival across Symi: classical and traditional music, theatre, dance, art exhibitions in venues like Panormitis monastery, the Symi Naval Museum, and various harborside squares. Refreshingly substantive for an island this small.

AlonnisosReligiousconfirm locally

Feast of Kyra Panagia Monastery (boat pilgrimage)

weekend around 1 September · Kyra Panagia islet

The Athonite monastery of the Nativity of the Theotokos on the uninhabited islet of Kyra Panagia, north-east of Alonnisos, keeps its feast with vespers on the Saturday and an Athonite-style liturgy on the Sunday around the turn of August-September; pilgrims come out by kaiki from Alonnisos, Skopelos, Skiathos and Volos. There is no tourist infrastructure on the islet, so you need a place on one of the boats organised from Patitiri and your own water and hat.

ChiosFood & harvest

Mastic harvest

August through October

Not a single-day festival but a season. Visit any of the 24 mastichochoria during this time and you'll see the cleaned ground beneath the schinos trees waiting to catch the resin tears. Tours from the Mastic Museum in Pyrgi.

PorosArts & culture

Synoriaka of Modern Greece (Kapodistrias days), Poros Town

last days of August, 4 days

Four days at the end of August (27-30 August 2026, 30 Aug-1 Sep 2024) commemorating the 1828 Poros Conference where the borders of the new Greek state were drawn under Kapodistrias: lectures, Navy band concerts, theatre on the waterfront and a visiting Navy ship open to the public. Free; the concerts on the quay are the evening draw.

ZakynthosReligiousconfirm locally

Litany of Osios Iosif Samakos, Gaitani

first Sunday after 29 August

The relic of Osios Iosif, brought from Crete when Candia fell in 1669, rests in the church of the Transfiguration in Gaitani, and on the first Sunday after 29 August the Metropolitan, clergy and village lead an evening procession with it through the streets of Gaitani, followed by a village celebration; the municipality's 2026 programme also lists a traditional dance evening in Gaitani on 3 September. Gaitani is 3 km from Zakynthos town on the Machairado road.

SifnosPanigiri

Panigiri of Agios Symeon (summit chapel), above Kamares

31 August – 1 September · Agios Symeon hill (Kamares)

The end-of-summer feast on the 476 m peak of Agios Symeon above Kamares: on the evening of 31 August people walk or drive up the track for vespers, then eat revithada and lamb at the monastery's long tables with a violin-laouto takimi. The chapel of Pantokratoras at the Platys Gialos–Chrysopigi junction celebrates the same night and is the easy-access alternative. Ask at your hotel whether the panigyras is laying on food that year — it depends on the sponsor.

AmmoulianiReligiousconfirm locally

Feast of Panagia Stylarini, Ammouliani

weekend after 14 September

Ammouliani was settled in 1925 by refugees from the island of Marmara, who brought the cult of their 14th-century icon of Panagia Stylarini (the original is in Koutloumousiou on Athos, the copy in the island's church). The feast is kept on the Saturday-Sunday after the Exaltation of the Cross (15-16 September 2018, 16-17 September 2023) with a great vespers, a procession down to the harbour and an episcopal liturgy with the Metropolitan of Ierissos. The season is winding down, so rooms are easy and the fish tavernas still open.

Naxos Potato Festival, Chora

a Saturday in September (12 September in 2026), previously held in summer · Naxos Chora

The Union of Agricultural Cooperatives of Naxos gives away tonnes of the island's famous potatoes fried, boiled and baked on the harbour square in Chora, with big-name Greek singers on stage and dancing until late; 2026 was the 17th edition and its 100th anniversary. SantoriniArts & culture

Ifestia volcano festival

a Saturday in the second half of September (21 Sep 2024, 19 Sep 2026) · Fira (caldera rim) / Nea Kameni

The municipality's re-enactment of the Minoan eruption: for over thirty years a huge fireworks display has been fired from the volcano of Nea Kameni after dark, with concerts on the caldera rim in Fira. SifnosFood & harvest

Cycladic Gastronomy Festival 'Nikolaos Tselementes', Artemonas

mid-September, 3 days (17–19 Sep 2026)

Named after the Sifnos-born cookbook author Tselementes, this three-day festival run by the Sifnos Cultural Society and the municipality brings cooks from other Cyclades islands and abroad to Artemonas for evening tastings, cooking demonstrations and music; the 18th edition is 17–19 September 2026. Tastings are free once the presentations end, and the queues are long — go on the first night. Book a room early, as September is already busy on Sifnos.

SpetsesReligious

Feast of Agios Mamas and blessing of the horses, Dapia

1–2 September · Spetses Town (Dapia)

The tiny chapel of Agios Mamas stands on the beach by the Dapia, built by a captain saved from a storm in 1850 while carrying grape must; on the evening of 1 September, after vespers, the island's horse-carriages line up and the priest blesses the horses and their drivers, and the story of the vow is retold. Ten minutes, free, and one of the most Spetsiot things you can see; it falls the week before the Armata.

Panigiri of Ai-Giannis Siderianos, Chalakas

25–26 September

The last big feast of the Milos season, for Agios Ioannis Theologos 'Siderianos' at the old monastery in the wild south-west (Chalakas). The icon is brought over from the Korfiatissa church with honours, and the village and the church committee lay on a glenti with island musicians in the courtyard; the party is usually held on the Saturday nearest the feast (20 Sept 2025, 21 Sept 2024) with vespers on 25 September, so check milosvoice.gr for the exact night. Hundreds come, including Kimolians; the road in is unpaved for the last stretch.

Panigiri of Agios Ioannis Theologos, Diakofti

25–26 September

The hill chapel of Agios Ioannis at Diakofti celebrates the Theologian on 26 September (and again on 8 May) with a family-run panigiri: vespers, then lamb, wine and tsambouna music for whoever climbs up. September on Mykonos is when the old island reappears, and this is a good place to see it.

Panigiri of Agios Ioannis Theologos, Apollonas

25–26 September

The fishing village of Apollonas at the northern tip celebrates St John the Theologian twice, on 8 May and 26 September, with grilled meat and seafood, local wine, music and dancing by the harbour. The September one is the last panigiri of the Naxos season, with the tourists gone and the water still warm.

25–26 September

The monastery of Agios Ioannis Theologos just below Nikia, the village on the crater rim, has its feast on 25-26 September: vespers, food in the courtyard and dancing to violin and laouto. Late season, so a good excuse to see the volcano with few people around.
