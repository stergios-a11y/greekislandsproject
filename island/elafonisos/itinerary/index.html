Elafonisos in 2 Days

A short, focused escape to one of Greece's finest beaches — Simos, the village harbour and the quiet coves of the north coast.

Elafonisos in 2 days, day by day: what to see, in what order, how far you drive and where you sleep. Base: Elafonisos Village. It works without a car.

🏖 Beaches on this plan: Simos Beach · Kato Nisi (Panagia) Beach — each links to its card with the wind rule and today’s conditions. All 3, ranked →
DAY 1

Simos Beach & the Village

5 km · 10 min 🌙 Sleep: Elafonisos Village
09:00

Elafonisos Ferry

Ferry from Pounta on the mainland — about 10 minutes across the strait. Cars can cross but the island is best explored on foot or by bicycle.

09:15

Elafonisos Village

The only settlement on the island — a small fishing village with whitewashed houses, a harbour, and a handful of tavernas and rooms. Quiet, genuine and unhurried.

10:00

Simos Beach 🏖 beach card · wind & conditions →

Consistently one of the best beaches in Europe — a double bay of pink-white sand with Caribbean-blue water, separated by a low sand dune. Both bays have a completely different character. The water colour is extraordinary.

18:00

Simos Sunset 🏖 beach card · wind & conditions →

Return to Simos for the late afternoon — the beach empties as day trippers leave, and the water turns from turquoise to gold. The best time of day.

20:30

Dinner in Elafonisos Village

Short drive back to the village (about 10 minutes from Simos). The harbour-front tavernas are unpretentious and famous for their fresh fish — sea bream, sea bass, and the catch of the day grilled simply with lemon and olive oil.

🍽 Lunch · Elafonisos Village Ourania is widely called the island's best — Ourania cooks as if for family; Sta Kala Kathoumena is the 1960s-feel family taverna known for oven-baked goat.
DAY 2

North Coast & Departure

4 km · 8 min
11:00

Kato Nisi (Panagia) Beach 🏖 beach card · wind & conditions →

The long west-coast beach — Kato Nisi, known locally as Panagia. Open water, clean sand and the best sunset on the island; usually near-empty because everyone is at Simos.

12:30

Simos — Final Swim 🏖 beach card · wind & conditions →

One final swim at Simos before the ferry back. The beach is quieter in the late morning before the day trippers arrive from the mainland.

15:00

Ferry to Mainland

Ferry back to Pounta. The 5-minute crossing is the shortest sea journey to leave one of Europe's best beaches behind.

Planning questions

Is 2 days enough for Elafonisos?

Yes, for a first visit. The plan covers the essentials at a pace that leaves time in the water. With one more day, add a slow beach day with no driving.

Do I need a car on Elafonisos?

No. This plan works on foot, by boat and with the local bus.

Where should I stay on Elafonisos?

The plan sleeps in Elafonisos Village.
