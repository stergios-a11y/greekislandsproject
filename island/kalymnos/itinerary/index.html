Kalymnos in 3 Days

Pothia's sponge heritage, the climbing villages of the west coast, and the beaches and caves of Telendos.

Kalymnos in 3 days, day by day: what to see, in what order, how far you drive and where you sleep. Base: Masouri. It assumes a hire car from day one.

🏖 Beaches on this plan: Myrties Beach · Emborios Beach — each links to its card with the wind rule and today’s conditions. All 3, ranked →
DAY 1

Pothia & the Sponge Heritage

10 km · 15 min 🌙 Sleep: Masouri
09:00

Pothia

The capital — a working Greek port built on the slopes above a natural harbour. The Italian-era waterfront buildings are the finest in the Dodecanese outside Rhodes and Kos. Not pretty in a Cycladic sense but genuinely interesting.

10:00

Sponge Factory & Museum

Working sponge factories line the harbour — buy a genuine Kalymnos sponge. The small Nautical Museum tells the extraordinary and tragic story of the island's sponge-diving industry.

11:30

Archaeological Museum

Small but excellent museum including the Lady of Kalymnos, a magnificent 1st-century BC bronze statue recovered from the sea in 1994. A rare survival.

14:00

Chora (Old Capital)

The old Byzantine capital above Pothia — a medieval village with a Knights' castle and views over the island. Quieter and more atmospheric than the modern port.

17:00

Myrties Beach 🏖 beach card · wind & conditions →

After Chora, the natural detour west drops you at Myrties — the prettiest beach on the island, looking directly across the narrow channel to Telendos islet. Fine sand and pebbles, taverns set just back from the water, and the famous Telendos sunset as the cliffs opposite catch the last light. A perfect transition before returning to Pothia for dinner.

🍽 Dinner · Pothia The harbour tavernas do excellent fresh fish. In the backstreets, Xefteris is the island's old-timer (since 1933) for classic Kalymnian home cooking. At Linaria, Kapadiko is chef Michalis Marthas's return to his island — dry-aged fish, sea-urchin salad, a tasting menu at €110 or à la carte €50–70.
DAY 2

Masouri & the Climbing Coast

20 km · 30 min 🌙 Sleep: Masouri
09:30

Masouri

The climbing capital of Kalymnos — a lively village on the west coast entirely built around the sport. Shops selling gear, cafes full of climbers from every country. Even if you don't climb, it's a fascinating scene.

11:00

Grande Grotta

The largest and most famous climbing cave on Kalymnos — a huge overhanging limestone cavern above the coast. Free to visit. Watch the climbers above while swimming below.

13:00

Myrties Beach 🏖 beach card · wind & conditions →

Pebble beach next to Masouri, with the islet of Telendos directly opposite — one of the most dramatic views in the Dodecanese. Good tavernas on the waterfront.

16:00

Emborios Village 🏖 beach card · wind & conditions →

Traditional fishing village at the northern end of the island — small pebble beach, a handful of tavernas, and a refreshingly unhurried pace compared to Masouri.

🍽 Dinner · Masouri Aegean Tavern at the end of Masouri beach (since 1989) is the island's best — a modern take on Greek cooking; Thrimpi just beyond has a white terrace for the sunset.
DAY 3

Telendos Island & Departure

5 km · 15 min
10:00

Telendos Boat

Small boat from Myrties — the crossing takes 10 minutes. Telendos broke off from Kalymnos in an earthquake in 554 AD. No cars on the island.

10:15

Telendos Village

A tiny car-free village with a few tavernas, a small hotel and the best pebble beach on Kalymnos. Excellent lunch at one of the waterfront tavernas.

11:30

Hochlakas Beach

Pebble beach at Telendos — crystal clear water, no crowds, and views back to Kalymnos. One of the best swimming spots in the Dodecanese.

16:30

Pothia — Departure

Ferry to Kos (frequent), Leros, Patmos, Piraeus or Rhodes. Kalymnos has its own small airport with Athens flights.

Planning questions

Is 3 days enough for Kalymnos?

Yes, for a first visit. The plan covers the essentials at a pace that leaves time in the water. With one more day, add a slow beach day with no driving.

Do I need a car on Kalymnos?

Yes. The beaches and villages are spread out and the bus does not reach the ones that matter; book ahead for July and August.

Where should I stay on Kalymnos?

The plan sleeps in Masouri.
