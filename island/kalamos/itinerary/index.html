Kalamos in 1 day, day by day: what to see, in what order, how far you drive and where you sleep. Base: Kalamos village. It works without a car.

DAY 1

Village, Pines & Port Leone

12 km · 50 min 🌙 Sleep: Kalamos village
09:00

Rent a boat in Mytikas

The best way to do these islands is to skip the timetable and drive yourself. Nemo Rent a Boat and others on the Mytikas quay hire small outboards for around €100 a day, plus roughly €50 of fuel — and no boat licence is needed, an ordinary car licence is enough. The distances here are tiny, so with an early start and a calm forecast you can take in Kalamos, Kastos and a string of empty coves in a single day. Watch the wind: the afternoon breeze gets up, so plan to be back before it does. Riding your own boat between these islands is the thing people remember from the trip.

10:30

Kalamos village & harbour

The main village climbs steeply from a small harbour that fills with yachts every summer evening. Whitewashed lanes, a couple of tavernas and shops, and pines coming right down to the water.

12:30

Episkopi

The island's second, much quieter village looks across a narrow channel to Mytikas on the mainland. A handful of houses, a small quay and absolute calm — the road there winds through pine forest with sea views the whole way.

15:00

Port Leone

An abandoned village on a deep, sheltered bay in the island's south — its people left after the 1953 earthquake broke the water supply, but the church is still cared for. No road: reach it by boat or on foot along the old path. One of the most atmospheric anchorages in the Ionian.

🍽 Lunch · Episkopi Varka sits right on the water at Episkopi — the pick on Kalamos. Grilled fish off the day's boats, goat, horta and the island's own oil, eaten at tables a few metres from where the yachts tie up. Short menu, changes with the catch; worth calling ahead in August.

Planning questions

Is 1 day enough for Kalamos?

Yes, for a first visit. The plan covers the essentials at a pace that leaves time in the water. With one more day, add a slow beach day with no driving.

Do I need a car on Kalamos?

No. This plan works on foot, by boat and with the local bus.

Where should I stay on Kalamos?

The plan sleeps in Kalamos village.
