Day 1~40 km
DAY 1

Aegina Town, Aphaea, Perdika & Moni

40 km · 60 min
09:30

Aegina Town

Arrive by ferry from Piraeus (40 min hydrofoil). The harbour is lined with painted fishing boats and pistachio sellers. The Tower of Markellos in the old town is worth a walk.

10:15

Temple of Apollo (Kolona)

A single standing column marks the temple of Apollo on a low hill overlooking the harbour. Small but atmospheric site with an excellent museum.

11:30

Temple of Aphaea

One of the three temples forming the 'sacred triangle' with the Parthenon and Poseidon at Sounio. 5th century BC, outstandingly preserved, on a pine-covered hilltop with sea views on every side. Unmissable.

13:30

Perdika

The fishing village at the island's southwest tip: a short harbour lined with fish tavernas looking straight at Moni. Lunch here, then take the small boat across.

15:00

Moni 🏖 beach card · wind & conditions →

Ten minutes by small boat from Perdika (some boats also run from Aegina Town in summer). Swim at the pine-shaded cove — the best water of the day — and look out for the peacocks and deer. Check the time of the last boat back.

17:45

Marathonas Beach 🏖 beach card · wind & conditions →

On the way back to town: a late swim on the sand, or a drink at the beach tavernas as the sun drops.

19:30

Aegina — Departure

Ferry back to Piraeus — frequent departures until late evening.

🍽 Lunch · Aegina Town & Perdika Agora is the fish-market ouzeri in Aegina Town. Worth the drive to Nontas in the fishing village of Perdika (family-run since 1936). For the locals' pick, Vatzoulias does taverna classics in a jasmine garden.
🌙 Evening If you linger past the day-trip, Aegina Town's waterfront is chilled and stylish rather than wild. Speak Low is a speakeasy-style cocktail bar (the Moscow Mules are the move); Riva Lounge does premium drinks and tapas in a prime spot. Most bars wind down by 2am.

Planning questions

Is 1 day enough for Aegina?

Yes, for a first visit. The plan covers the essentials at a pace that leaves time in the water. With one more day, add a slow beach day with no driving.

Do I need a car on Aegina?

It helps for a day or two, but the core of this plan works with the bus, taxis and boats.

Where should I stay on Aegina?

Pick a base near the port for the first and last night.
