DAY 1

Hydra Town & Vlychos

5 km · 45 min 🌙 Sleep: Hydra Town
09:00

Hydra Harbour

Arrive and settle into the rhythm. No cars means no noise — just donkeys carrying luggage up the lanes. The harbour is one of the most beautiful in Greece.

10:00

Historical Archive Museum

Small but excellent museum in a neoclassical mansion on the harbour — the story of Hydra's extraordinary role in the Greek War of Independence. The island provided more ships than any other.

11:30

Spilia Swimming Rocks

The town's swimming spot — rocky platforms right below the Spilia café. Crystal clear water and cliff-jumping. 5 minutes from the harbour.

15:30

Vlychos Beach 🏖 beach card · wind & conditions →

Pebble beach 30 minutes walk along the coastal path. Clear water, sunbeds, and the excellent Taverna Marina right behind the beach. The last beach reachable on foot.

20:30

Dinner in Hydra Town

Walk back along the coast path or take a water taxi (10 minutes). The harbour at twilight is the postcard image of Hydra — find a table at one of the seafront tavernas.

🍽 Lunch · Kamini Castello is a harbourfront taverna in the fishing hamlet of Kamini; Kodylenia's sits at the edge of the same harbour with a waterfront terrace.
🍽 Dinner · Hydra Town Gitoniko (Manolis & Christina) is the island institution — honest home cooking and a rooftop garden, worth seeking out through the lanes.
🌙 Evening No cars and no clubs — on Hydra the whole port becomes the party and you drift between bars. Start with a sundowner at Hydronetta, out on the cannons above the sea, then Pirate Bar for the institution and Amalour for candlelit cocktails and dancing to soul and funk.
DAY 2

Bisti or Mandraki & Departure

8 km · 20 min
10:00

Bisti Beach 🏖 beach card · wind & conditions →

Secluded pine-fringed pebble beach on the west coast — accessible only by water taxi or beach boat from the harbour (20 min). A diving club operates here.

13:00

Hydra Town Evening

Return to town for lunch. Walk the upper lanes to find Leonard Cohen's house (No. 10 Merkouraki lane). The antiquarian bookshops are excellent.

16:00

Hydra — Departure

Ferry to Piraeus (90 min) or to the other Saronic islands.

Planning questions

Is 2 days enough for Hydra?

Yes, for a first visit. The plan covers the essentials at a pace that leaves time in the water. With one more day, add a slow beach day with no driving.

Do I need a car on Hydra?

No. This plan works on foot, by boat and with the local bus.

Where should I stay on Hydra?

The plan sleeps in Hydra Town.
