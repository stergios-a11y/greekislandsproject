Rhodes

4.4/5

The island of knights, ancient acropolis and emerald bays

📍 1,400 km²👥 115,000🗓 5 days✈ Airport🚗 Essential

From the medieval streets of Rhodes Town to the white lanes of Lindos, the knights' castles of the interior and the wild southern tip — a complete circuit of the island.

Overall rating: 4.4/5 · 1400 km² · 115000 residents

Updated: 27/Aug/2026

The largest of the Dodecanese, Rhodes packs an extraordinary range into one island: a UNESCO-listed medieval city, the cliff-top Acropolis of Lindos, castle ruins at dramatic viewpoints, mountain villages making wine and honey, and some of the finest beaches in Greece along its sheltered east coast. More than 300 days of sunshine a year make it the sunniest place in the country.

Getting there

✈ Airport (intl)⛵ Piraeus 13–18h€55–180

Largest Dodecanese airport — Aegean, Sky Express, Ryanair, charters. Ferries from Piraeus dock at Rhodes Town port — Blue Star overnight, daily, €55–95 deck or €100–180 cabin.

Read full route

Direct flights are usually faster and similar cost to a cabin.

Tip: Daily summer hydrofoil to Marmaris (Turkey) takes ~1h — easy add-on.

Best time to visit Rhodes

Rhodes has two seasons inverted: the medieval Old Town is best off-season (October-April) when you can walk it without bumping into a cruise group; the beaches and Lindos are best May-October. June and September are when both work. August is German-package peak and Old Town becomes a queue. The Knights' Palace deserves an early-morning visit.

5-day itinerary for Rhodes

Day 1: Rhodes Old Town & the North

Overnight: Rhodes Town · Drive: 20 km, ~30 min

  1. 09:00 · Mandraki Harbour
    The ancient harbour where the Colossus of Rhodes once stood. Start your day with coffee watching the sailboats.
  2. 10:00 · Palace of the Grand Master
    The finest example of Gothic architecture in Greece. Former seat of the Knights of St John. Impressive mosaics inside.
  3. 11:30 · Street of the Knights
    The best-preserved medieval street in Europe. Each section was occupied by a different langue of the Knights Hospitaller.
  4. 16:30 · Kallithea Springs
    Stunning Italian-built thermal spa complex from the 1920s on the coast. Beautiful architecture and a clear swimming cove.
  5. 18:00 · Elli Beach
    Town beach just north of the Old Town. Evening swim, then dinner in the old town.
  6. 🍴 Stop for dinner · Rhodes Old Town — see Eat & Drink below
Eat & Drink
🍴
Dinner · Rhodes Old Town
The Old Town is the night's setting. Marco Polo Café is the upscale choice — the candlelit courtyard of a restored Ottoman-quarter mansion, consistently rated among the island's best. Nireas is the family-run seafood institution (impeccable service, fair prices), and Hatzikelis serves celebrated seafood among ancient stone ruins.
🍸
Nightlife
Rhodes Town splits in two after dark. The medieval Old Town hides candle-lit wine bars and rooftop lounges in its stone alleys — perfect for an early drink. Then the new town's Bar Street (Orfanidou) is the loud strip: Colorado Club and Gazi anchor it, and nothing fills until midnight. Package-resort Faliraki, 14km south, is the other option.

Day 2: West Side — Kamiros, Butterflies & Kritinia

Overnight: Rhodes Town · Drive: 85 km, ~95 min

  1. 09:00 · Kritinia Castle
    🚗 ≈1 hr drive from Rhodes Town
    Ruined Knights' castle on a hilltop with panoramic sea views toward Turkey. Free entry. Small cafe at the base with wine and honey tastings.
  2. 10:30 · Valley of the Butterflies
    Unique valley where thousands of Jersey tiger moths gather in summer on the sweet storax trees. Shaded walks beside streams.
  3. 12:30 · Ancient Kamiros
    Ruins of one of the three ancient city-states of Rhodes — temples, stoas and a Hellenistic reservoir. Far less visited than Lindos but equally impressive.
  4. 🍴 Stop for lunch · Embonas wine village — see Eat & Drink below
  5. 17:00 · Rhodes Town
    Return to Rhodes Town for the night. Dinner in the Old Town.
Eat & Drink
🍴
Lunch · Embonas wine village
Worth the short inland detour to the wine village of Embonas under Mount Attavyros. Taverna Savvas and Bakis both do great-value spreads of grilled local meat with the village's famous wine; Maroulakis is the quieter local favourite off the tour-bus track. Prefer the coast? Kamiros Beach Taverna is right on the route.
🍸
Nightlife
Back in Rhodes Town — the Old Town has plenty beyond the Bar Street strip. Macao Bar mixes 20-odd cocktails around a 13m watermill in its courtyard and draws the well-dressed crowd; Sissitio pours creative drinks among ancient walls and cobbled lanes.

Day 3: The South — Monolithos & Siana

Overnight: Rhodes Town · Drive: 140 km, ~150 min

  1. 08:30 · Rhodes Town
    Early departure heading south down the west coast — the longest driving day of the trip.
  2. 10:00 · Glystra Beach
    🚗 ≈1 hr drive south from Rhodes Town
    Sheltered sandy cove in the south with shallow, warm water. One of the least crowded excellent beaches on the island. Add 80 km round-trip if you want to push on to Prasonisi at the southern tip — a dramatic sandbar where the Aegean and Mediterranean meet, world-class for windsurfing.
  3. 15:00 · Siana Village
    🚗 ≈40 min up into the mountains
    Mountain village famous for souma (local spirit) and thyme honey. Stop at a local shop for tastings — both are exceptional.
  4. 16:30 · Monolithos Castle
    15th century Knights' castle perched on a 240-metre rock pinnacle with sheer drop views to the sea. The finest sunset viewpoint on Rhodes.
  5. 🍴 Stop for lunch · Monolithos — see Eat & Drink below
  6. 21:30 · Evening in Rhodes Town
    Long drive back from the southwest to Rhodes Town (about 1.5 hours). The journey itself is part of the day — through the mountain villages and back along the coast as evening falls.
Eat & Drink
🍴
Lunch · Monolithos
In the cliff-top village of Monolithos. Christos Corner is the warm one — Christos walks you to the fridge to pick your fish or meat for the charcoal. Old Monolithos does mezze on a panoramic terrace, and Taverna Panorama earns its name with the view.
🍸
Nightlife
Another Old Town night, different corners. Soho Bar by the fountain on Ippokratous Square is the classic start to a Rhodes bar-crawl; Monkey Cocktail Bar is the hidden-gem spot for adventurous, bold-flavoured cocktails.

Day 4: East Coast → Lindos

Overnight: Lindos · Drive: 75 km, ~90 min

  1. 09:00 · Rhodes Town
    Depart south along the scenic east coast road.
  2. 10:00 · Anthony Quinn Bay
    🚗 ≈20 min from Rhodes Town
    Iconic emerald cove named after the actor who filmed The Guns of Navarone here. Extraordinary clarity — bring a snorkel.
  3. 11:30 · Ladiko Beach
    Secluded pebble bay just 5 minutes walk from Anthony Quinn. Rocky seabed excellent for snorkelling, fewer crowds.
  4. 13:30 · Tsambika Beach
    The best family beach on Rhodes — long golden sand, shallow water, excellent facilities. Lunch at one of the beach tavernas.
  5. 15:30 · Tsambika Monastery
    Climb the 300 steps to the hilltop Byzantine monastery for sweeping views over the coast and beach below.
  6. 17:00 · Agathi Beach
    Golden sand and crystal clear water beneath Feraklos Castle. One of the finest beaches on the island. Morning swim before the crowds arrive.
  7. 18:30 · Lindos
    Arrive in Lindos in the late afternoon. Check in, then walk down to the village for dinner with the Acropolis lit up above.
  8. 🍴 Stop for dinner · Lindos — see Eat & Drink below
Eat & Drink
🍴
Dinner · Lindos
You're staying in Lindos — make tonight count. Mavrikos is the island's most celebrated table, run by the same family since 1933 and listed in the Greek Gastronomy Guide; book ahead. Melenos is the upscale rooftop with candlelit Acropolis views, and Kalypso is the family-run Captain's House with a rooftop terrace and great value.
🍸
Nightlife
Lindos is the calm counterpoint. As the Acropolis lights up on the rock above, the village's rooftop bars come into their own — Ktima Lindos looks out over Vlycha Bay. Drinks and a long dinner under the floodlit citadel, not a club night.

Day 5: Lindos & Departure

Drive: 70 km, ~80 min

  1. 11:00 · Lindos Acropolis
    3rd century BC acropolis with a Doric temple of Athena Lindia perched above a sheer 116-metre cliff. One of the most dramatic ancient sites in Greece.
  2. 12:30 · Lindos Village
    Dazzling white cubist houses in maze-like lanes. Browse the galleries and craftshops before lunch.
  3. 🍴 Stop for lunch · Lindos — see Eat & Drink below
  4. 16:00 · St Paul's Bay
    Heart-shaped bay just south of Lindos with crystal water and the Acropolis looming above. One of the most beautiful spots in Greece.
  5. 17:00 · Rhodes Town — Departure
    Drive back to Rhodes Town for ferry or flight departure. Final coffee at Mandraki harbour.
Eat & Drink
🍴
Lunch · Lindos
A last rooftop lunch over the village. Mythos arguably has the best view of all — the Acropolis on one side, the sea on the other. Dionysos does a big range of Greek dishes on a breezy terrace. For the famous Mavrikos, lunch is the quieter time to get a table.

Top beaches of Rhodes

Tsambika Beach

The finest beach on Rhodes — a sweeping arc of golden sand framed by mountains, with the hilltop monastery above. Exceptionally shallow and calm water making it ideal for families. The best all-round beach on the island.

Type
Fine golden sand
Length
1.2 km
Depth
Shallow — very gradual entry, safe for children
Wind protection
East-facing — mostly sheltered from the meltemi (the summer N/NE wind); can be choppy on the strongest NE days
Facilities
Fully organised: sunbeds, umbrellas, water sports, beach bars, tavernas, showers, parking

Anthony Quinn Bay

The most iconic cove on Rhodes, named after the actor who filmed The Guns of Navarone here and briefly owned the bay. The water is an otherworldly emerald-green colour. Best for snorkelling — the rocky seabed teems with fish. Arrive early; it fills up quickly.

Type
Pebble and rock
Length
150 m
Depth
Medium to deep — rocky seabed, exceptional water clarity
Wind protection
East-facing — mostly sheltered from the meltemi (the summer N/NE wind); can be choppy on the strongest NE days
Facilities
Basic: one beach bar, sunbeds. No parking on beach — park above and walk down.

St Paul's Bay (Agios Pavlos)

A near-perfectly enclosed bay just south of Lindos with the Acropolis visible on the cliff above. Described by many visitors as the most beautiful beach setting in Greece. The combination of crystal water, white rock and the ancient ruins overhead is unforgettable.

Type
Sand and fine pebble
Length
300 m
Depth
Shallow to medium — calm, clear water
Wind protection
Northeast-facing — exposed to the meltemi (the dominant summer N/NE wind); often windy on meltemi days
Facilities
Organised: sunbeds and umbrellas (pricey), beach bar, water taxi from Lindos

Agathi Beach

A beautiful crescent of fine golden sand in the shadow of the ruined Feraklos Castle near Haraki. Clear, shallow water and fewer crowds than the main tourist beaches. One of the best-kept secrets on the east coast.

Type
Fine golden sand
Length
600 m
Depth
Shallow — sandy bottom, ideal for families
Wind protection
East-facing — mostly sheltered from the meltemi (the summer N/NE wind); can be choppy on the strongest NE days
Facilities
Good: sunbeds, umbrellas, beach bar, taverna. Feraklos Castle visible above.

Glystra Beach

A sheltered sandy cove in the relatively undeveloped south of Rhodes near Kiotari. Crystal clear, warm, shallow water and a relaxed family atmosphere. Far fewer tourists than the northern beaches. A superb half-day stop on the southern loop.

Type
Fine sand
Length
400 m

Shallow — very safe, calm water
Wind protection
East-facing — mostly sheltered from the meltemi (the summer N/NE wind); can be choppy on the strongest NE days
Facilities
Basic: small family beach cafe, some sunbeds. Quiet and unspoiled.

Local & Seasonal

🎉Festivals & Events

Medieval Rose FestivalOctober (typically 2nd weekend)
Living-history festival inside the medieval Old Town (UNESCO site): jousting knights, archery, sword fighting, fire shows, medieval markets, theatre. Reenacts life under the Order of Saint John (1310-1522). Run by the non-profit Medieval Rose since 2007. Held in October since 2023 — recent editions have been on the 2nd weekend (e.g. 10-12 Oct 2025).
