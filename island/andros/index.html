Gavrio, Zorkos, neoclassical Chora and Achla

Overall rating: 3.7/5 · 380 km² · 9200 residents

Why 3.7: easy access and real history and character — but it is not one of the cheap islands.

Updated: 08/Nov/2026

Andros is the Cycladic island with running streams, oak forest and a 100 km waymarked trail. It is the northernmost and second-largest Cycladic island, but it feels like nowhere else in the Cyclades: spring-fed water, wooded valleys and stone-paved paths make it more Tuscan than Aegean. The main town, Chora, is elegantly neoclassical — a sea-captains' town on a narrow promontory with a marble-paved main street and the Goulandris Museum, the largest contemporary art museum in the Aegean, with Modigliani, Picasso and Giacometti in the permanent collection. Inland at Apikia you can fill a bottle at the public fountain that supplies Sariza, Greece's best-known bottled spring water, then walk 15 minutes through oak forest to the cold pools at the Pythara waterfalls. Eat fourtalia, the island omelette with potatoes, local sausage and wild fennel. Skip it if you depend on Piraeus — Andros sails only from Rafina — or if you want everything close together: the ferry docks at Gavrio, 35 km from the lively southeast. September is the best month, sea and walks both; August is when the Athens summer-house traffic is at its worst.

Good for

  • Hikers — the 100 km Andros Route crosses streams, oak forest and stone-paved trails
  • Travellers who want a green, almost Tuscan island unlike the rest of the Cyclades
  • Anyone who likes a cultured base — neoclassical Chora has the Aegean's largest modern-art museum

Maybe skip if

  • If you depend on Piraeus — Andros sails only from Rafina
  • If you want everything close together; the port and the lively southeast are 35 km apart

Getting there

⛵ Rafina 2h€18–35

No airport, no ferry from Piraeus. Andros is served exclusively from Rafina (1h east of Athens by car/bus).

Ferries dock at Gavrio on the northwest coast — 35km from the lively southeast.

Tip: KTEL bus from Athens (Mavromateon terminal) goes straight to Rafina port — no need to drive.

Best time to visit Andros

Andros is the Cyclades island that doesn't feel like the Cyclades — green, watered, hiking trails everywhere. Swim May through October; hike March through November. The Andros Marathon walks (organised hiking events) run May and October. Avoid August only if you dislike crowds; Athens summer-house traffic is intense.

3-day itinerary for Andros

Read the day-by-day plan on its own page →

Day 1: Gavrio & the North

Overnight: Batsi · Drive: 50 km, ~85 min

  1. 10:00 · Gavrio (arrival)
    The ferry from Rafina lands here, so the trip starts here. Pick up the hire car at the port (book ahead in July–August), have a coffee on the waterfront and head north. Chrissi Ammos, the long organised sand beach, is 3 km south if you'd rather ease in.
  2. 10:45 · Tower of Agios Petros
    A 20-metre round tower of the Hellenistic period, 5 minutes' drive above Gavrio — one of the best-preserved ancient towers in Greece. Free, open site; 15 minutes is enough, and the view over the bay of Gavrio is a bonus.
  3. 12:00 · Zorkos
    Wide bay of golden sand and turquoise water on the far northeast, about 35 minutes from Gavrio; the last stretch is a broad dirt road signposted 'Aegea', fine for an ordinary car. Sunbeds, showers and the Zorkos fish taverna on the bay — swim, have lunch here and stay into the afternoon. It deepens quickly; snorkelling along the rocks at either end is good.
  4. 🍴 Stop for lunch · Zorkos — see Eat & Drink below
  5. 18:30 · Batsi (sunset)
    West-coast port village with a curved sandy bay — the island's liveliest evening. Sunset on the waterfront, dinner at the harbour, then the bars along the promenade. A good first-night base: it's 15 minutes from Gavrio.
  6. 🍴 Stop for dinner · Batsi — see Eat & Drink below

Day 2: Across to Chora

Overnight: Chora · Drive: 33 km, ~55 min

  1. 10:00 · Palaiopoli
    The ancient capital of Andros, on the slope between the main road and the sea. Stop at the small archaeological collection by the road and the viewpoint over the excavations; the walk down to the ancient harbour is long, so most people stop at the top. 30–40 minutes. For a swim, Apothikes and Chalkolimnionas, two small sandy coves, are a few kilometres south (turn off at Stavropeda; the last stretch is dirt).
  2. 11:15 · Menites
    Shaded village on the slope above the Mesaria valley, built around its springs: water runs from six marble lion heads beside the church of Panagia Koumoulou, said to stand on an ancient temple of Dionysus. Fill a bottle, sit at the kafenio under the plane trees, then drive the 10 minutes down to Chora.
  3. 11:45 · Ano Mesaria
    A minute below Menites, on the way to Chora: the 12th-century Byzantine church of the Taxiarches, with restored frescoes, and a kafenio under the plane tree on the square. A short stop.
  4. 12:30 · Andros Town (Chora)
    Neoclassical sea-captains' town on a narrow promontory between two beaches. The marble-paved main street runs from the inland square down to Kairis square at the tip; lunch here, then the museums. Come back at the end of the day for the lighthouse.
  5. 🍴 Stop for lunch · Chora — see Eat & Drink below
  6. 14:30 · Archaeological Museum
    Small but excellent archaeological museum, home to the famous Hermes of Andros — a Roman copy of a 4th-century BC original found on the island. The museum sits a short walk from the main square in the lower Chora; allow 30-40 minutes.
  7. 15:30 · Goulandris Museum of Contemporary Art
    Major contemporary art museum, founded by the Goulandris shipping family — the Aegean's most ambitious. Hosts world-class summer exhibitions that bring busloads of Athenians; the permanent collection has Modigliani, Picasso, Giacometti, and the museum's pride, sculptures by local-born Michalis Tombros. Check what's on before you go.
  8. 17:00 · Paraporti Beach
    Sandy beach right below Chora — walk down the cliff stairs and you're on the sand in five minutes. Family-friendly, calm water sheltered by the headland, and a single seasonal canteen for coffee and ice cream. The contrast between the neoclassical town above and the beach below is the postcard image of Andros.
  9. 19:30 · Kato Kastro & Tourlitis Lighthouse
    End the Chora day at its tip: past Kairis square and the Unknown Sailor statue, cross the stone bridge to the ruins of the Venetian Kato Kastro. Tourlitis, the lighthouse standing on a sea-rock just offshore, is right in front of you — best at sunset.
Eat & Drink
🍴
Lunch · Chora
Nonna's in Andros Town (Chora) is the reliable favourite.
🍸
Nightlife
Andros Chora is small but surprisingly switched-on. Moomba Afterlight is the open-air cocktail spot over the marina; Skyline does drinks from a rooftop over the town lights; and Andros Club is the proper late-night dance floor when the shipowners' kids are home for summer.

Day 3: Stenies, Pythara & Achla

Departure · Drive: 78 km, ~180 min

  1. 09:00 · Stenies
    Lush green sea-captains' village 5 km from Chora, set in a wooded valley with running streams — completely unlike a Cycladic landscape. The neoclassical mansions were built by Andros shipowners returning from Constantinople and Odessa in the 19th century; many are still occupied by their descendants.
  2. 09:45 · Apikia
    Mountain village 6 km inland from Chora, in a green valley fed by springs. The famous Sariza springs bottle Andros' drinking water — you can drink straight from the public fountain, and the locals do, daily. Stop at a kafenio for a coffee made with that water; the difference is real.
  3. 10:15 · Pythara Waterfalls
    Natural pools fed by waterfalls, reached on a 15-minute walk through dense oak forest from Apikia. The trail is shaded all the way and even in August stays cool. The pools themselves are cold enough that a full swim takes nerve — most people just sit on the rocks with their feet in. Worth it.
  4. 11:15 · Agios Nikolaos Monastery
    On the way up from Apikia towards Vourkoti and Achla: a monastery on the green hillside, with wide views over the valley down to the sea. Dress modestly; 20 minutes.
  5. 12:30 · Achla
    The island's finest beach: a long bay of white pebbles under high cliffs, where the Achla stream comes out through plane trees into emerald water, with the chapel of Agios Nikolaos at one end. The road from Vourkoti is dirt and slow — allow about an hour from Chora and drive carefully — or take one of the summer boat trips from Chora. Nothing to buy for most of the year: bring water and food.
  6. 18:30 · Gavrio (ferry back)
    From Achla back up to Vourkoti and across the island to Gavrio: allow about 1 h 30. Check your ferry time the day before and leave the beach in time.

Local & Seasonal

🍽Local Specialties

Fourtalia
Andriotic omelette with potatoes, local sausage, and wild fennel — heartier than the Tinian version. Try at Nonna's in Chora or the village tavernas of Apoikia.
Sariza spring water
Greece's most famous bottled spring water comes from Apoikia. You can drink it directly from the public tap in the village. Bring an empty bottle.
Kaltsounia
Sweet cheese pies — small fried pastries filled with fresh cheese and honey. A breakfast or dessert specialty. Best at the kafeneia of Apoikia and Aprovato.
Amygdalota
Dense almond cookies with rose water — Andriotic in style, slightly different from the Hydraiotika. Sold at every bakery in Chora.
