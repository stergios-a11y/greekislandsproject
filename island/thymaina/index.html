A working fishing island in the Fournoi archipelago — off-grid coves near Ikaria

The fishing port, the hamlet of Keramidou, and a swim in a quiet cove.

Overall rating: 3.0/5 · 10 km² · 191 residents

Last updated: July 6, 2026

Thymaina lies just west of Fournoi in the archipelago between Ikaria and Samos — a rugged little island that remains, above all, a working fishing community rather than a resort. Its two hamlets (the port settlement and Keramidou) are connected by a handful of steep lanes; the coast is a series of quiet pebble coves reached on foot or by boat. There is a school, a couple of tavernas, and a strong sense of everyday island life carrying on regardless of tourism. It rewards travellers who like their islands genuinely undiscovered.

Getting there

⛵ 10 min from Fournoi€3–6

No airport. A small local ferry crosses from Fournoi to Thymaina in about 10 minutes, several times a week; reach Fournoi itself by ferry from Ikaria, Samos or Piraeus. Off-season crossings are infrequent.

Read full route

Tip: Combine with Fournoi — Thymaina is a short hop and the two share the same ferry world.

When to Visit

A summer island reached via Fournoi — tavernas and rooms are busiest July to September, when Greek families and a few walkers arrive. June and September are calm and warm; outside summer, life is entirely local and boats are scarce.

1-day itinerary for Thymaina

Day 1: Port, Keramidou & a Cove Swim

Overnight: Thymaina port · Drive: 6 km, ~40 min

  1. 10:30 · Thymaina port
    The main settlement gathers around a working fishing harbour — caïques, nets drying, a couple of tavernas and a kafeneio. The small ferry from Fournoi ties up here.
  2. 🍴 Stop for lunch · Thymaina port — see Eat & Drink below
  3. 12:00 · Keramidou
    The second hamlet, a cluster of houses on the slopes with long views to Ikaria. A short, steep walk from the port through terraced hillsides — the whole island opens up along the way.
  4. 14:00 · Agios Nikolaos cove
    One of several quiet pebble coves along the sheltered coast, with clear water and no facilities. Reach it on foot or ask a fisherman for a lift by boat.
Eat & Drink
🍴
Lunch · Thymaina port
A couple of tavernas at the port cook whatever the boats land — fish, sea urchin, wild greens and the local Fournoi lobster pasta when it's about. Simple, cheap, and as fresh as it gets.

Top beaches of Thymaina

Agios Nikolaos cove

A quiet, clear-water pebble cove on Thymaina's sheltered coast — one of several small swimming spots reached on foot or by boat, with no facilities and rarely another soul.
