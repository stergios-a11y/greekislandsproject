DAY 1

Spartochori & the Sea Caves

16 km · 15 min 🌙 Sleep: Vathy
10:00

Spartochori 🏖 beach card · wind & conditions →

The most beautiful village on Meganisi — a hillside settlement of white houses above the main harbour. Extraordinary views over the straits to Lefkada and the surrounding islands. The village feels genuinely untouched.

11:00

Spartochori Beach 🏖 beach card · wind & conditions →

The little beach directly below Spartochori — a strip of pale pebbles and clear green-blue water at the foot of the village cliff, with yachts swinging at anchor just offshore. An easy walk down for a first swim before the day's boat trip.

12:00

Papanikolis Cave

One of the largest sea caves in Greece — named after the submarine that sheltered here in WWII. Accessible by boat. The cave opens into a cathedral-like chamber with extraordinary blue light.

14:30

Ammoglossa Sandspit 🏖 beach card · wind & conditions →

A blond tongue of sand reaching into the channel at Meganisi's north-west tip — shallow, warm water on both sides and a view straight across to Lefkada. The island's most photogenic swim, best in the softer late-afternoon light.

17:00

Vathy Harbour

The main harbour village of Meganisi — a small cove lined with tavernas. Dinner here watching the boats. The freshest seafood on the island.

🍽 Dinner · Vathy Errikos is the island's most famous table — grilled fish, octopus and mussels; Rose Garden is the top-rated Mediterranean spot.
DAY 2

Katomeri & Departure

8 km · 12 min
09:30

Katomeri

The second village of Meganisi — quieter than Spartochori, with a beautiful square shaded by plane trees and a small church. Morning coffee here before exploring the south of the island.

10:30

Limonari Beach 🏖 beach card · wind & conditions →

Remote pebble beach on the south coast, accessible only by boat or a long walk. Crystal clear water and complete solitude.

16:00

Spartochori (Porto Spilia) — Ferry back to Nydri 🏖 beach card · wind & conditions →

End the loop back at Spartochori's little port, Porto Spilia, for a last coffee on the waterfront before the ferry across to Nydri on Lefkada. Arrive twenty minutes early in August — the boat fills with day-trippers and their scooters.

Planning questions

Is 2 days enough for Meganisi?

Yes, for a first visit. The plan covers the essentials at a pace that leaves time in the water. With one more day, add a slow beach day with no driving.

Do I need a car on Meganisi?

No. This plan works on foot, by boat and with the local bus.

Where should I stay on Meganisi?

The plan sleeps in Vathy.
