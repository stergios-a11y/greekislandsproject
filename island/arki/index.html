Arki is a speck of an island east of Patmos — one small settlement around the harbour, a handful of tavernas that also rent rooms, no cars, no bank and no ATM. It is reached by the small Dodecanese ferries that call between Samos, Patmos, Lipsi and Kalymnos. The draw is the near-total silence, the shallow turquoise coves of the south coast, and the neighbouring islet of Marathi with one of the finest beaches in the eastern Aegean. This is an island for travellers who genuinely want to disappear for a day or two.

Getting there

⛵ via Patmos/Lipsi€8–15

No airport. Small Dodecanese ferries and the Patmos–Lipsi day boats call at Arki several times a week in summer; the crossing from Patmos is about 1 hour. Very limited service outside June–September.

Read full route

Tip: Pair Arki with Lipsi or Patmos — few people come out here as a standalone trip.

Best time to visit Arki

Arki is a summer-only island — most rooms and tavernas open roughly May to September. June and September are ideal: warm sea, calm bays and almost nobody around. Ferries thin out sharply outside summer.

1-day itinerary for Arki

Day 1: Harbour, Coves & Marathi

Overnight: Port Augusta (Arki) · Drive: 6 km, ~40 min

  1. 10:00 · Port Augusta (Arki village)
    The island's only village wraps around a deep natural harbour — three or four tavernas with rooms, a tiny church, a couple of shops. Boats from Patmos and Lipsi tie up right in front of the tables.
  2. 11:30 · Tiganakia Coves
    A cluster of tiny shallow coves on the south coast with astonishing turquoise water over pale sand — the island's signature swim. Reached on foot or by small boat; no facilities, so bring water and shade.
  3. 🍴 Stop for lunch · Port Augusta — see Eat & Drink below
  4. 14:00 · Marathi islet
    A ten-minute boat hop to a near-uninhabited islet with a wide sweep of pale sand and two summer-only tavernas. One of the best beaches in the whole eastern Aegean, and almost always empty.
Eat & Drink
🍴
Lunch · Port Augusta
A handful of family tavernas ring the harbour, each with rooms above and the day's catch below — grilled fish, goat, and whatever the garden gave. Cash only, no reservations, no rush.

Top beaches of Arki

Tiganakia

Shallow turquoise coves on Arki's south coast — the island's best swim, reached on foot from the village. No sunbeds, no bar, total peace.
