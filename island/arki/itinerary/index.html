DAY 1

Harbour, Coves & Marathi

6 km · 40 min 🌙 Sleep: Port Augusta (Arki)
10:00

Port Augusta (Arki village)

The island's only village wraps around a deep natural harbour — three or four tavernas with rooms, a tiny church, a couple of shops. Boats from Patmos and Lipsi tie up right in front of the tables.

11:30

Tiganakia Coves 🏖 beach card · wind & conditions →

A cluster of tiny shallow coves on the south coast with astonishing turquoise water over pale sand — the island's signature swim. Reached on foot or by small boat; no facilities, so bring water and shade.

14:00

Marathi islet

A ten-minute boat hop to a near-uninhabited islet with a wide sweep of pale sand and two summer-only tavernas. One of the best beaches in the whole eastern Aegean, and almost always empty.

🍽 Lunch · Port Augusta A handful of family tavernas ring the harbour, each with rooms above and the day's catch below — grilled fish, goat, and whatever the garden gave. Cash only, no reservations, no rush. The one taverna, Trypas, sits right on the little harbour — €25–50 a head, April to October.

Planning questions

Is 1 day enough for Arki?

Yes, for a first visit. The plan covers the essentials at a pace that leaves time in the water. With one more day, add a slow beach day with no driving.

Do I need a car on Arki?

No. This plan works on foot, by boat and with the local bus.

Where should I stay on Arki?

The plan sleeps in Port Augusta (Arki).
