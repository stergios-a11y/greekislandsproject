Skyros in 3 Days

Wild horses, Achilles' island and a unique cultural isolation

Skyros in 3 days, day by day: what to see, in what order, how far you drive and where you sleep. Base: Skyros Town and Departure. It assumes a hire car from day one.

DAY 1

Skyros Town & Castle

10 km · 25 min 🌙 Sleep: Skyros Town
11:00

Linaria (port)

Tiny pretty port on the west coast. Arrival tradition: a ship's horn salutes every ferry. One hour from the main town.

12:30

Skyros Town (Chora)

Cycladic-style whitewashed village on a cliff, unique to the Sporades. The main marble-paved pedestrian street climbs to the castle.

14:00

Byzantine Castle of Skyros

Ancient acropolis turned Venetian/Byzantine fort. 360° views over the town and the Aegean.

17:00

Faltaits Folklore Museum

In a 19th-century mansion. Skyrian embroidery, carved wooden furniture, Byzantine icons. Best folk museum in Greece.

19:30

Magazia Beach (sunset)

Long sandy beach right below Chora. Walk down for a swim before dinner.

🍽 Lunch · Skyros Town (Chora) Anemos is the small taverna with a panoramic terrace — excellent lobster pasta and Skyrian plates.
🍽 Dinner · Skyros Town (Chora) Margetis is the most authentic taverna in town — grilled meats and Greek wine; O Pappous ki ego does Skyrian stews and tapas-style plates.
DAY 2

North: Palamari & Beaches

40 km · 100 min 🌙 Sleep: Skyros Town
10:00

Molos Beach

Continuation of Magazia beach. Organised with umbrellas, tavernas; becomes wilder further north.

12:00

Palamari Archaeological Site

Early Bronze Age settlement (2500 BC). Small but important site with defensive walls still visible.

14:00

Atsitsa Beach 🏖 beach card · wind & conditions →

Pine-shaded pebble beach on the west coast. Hippy vibe, yoga retreat nearby. Lunch at the beach taverna.

16:30

Pefkos Beach

Shallow sandy bay backed by a pine forest. Calm, family-friendly.

20:30

Drive back to Skyros Town

Drive back to Chora (about 30 minutes from Pefkos). Skyros' main square fills up after dark — try a traditional taverna in the cubic-white village under the castle.

DAY 3

Kalamitsa, Skyrian Horses & Linaria

50 km · 120 min 🌙 Sleep: Departure
09:30

Kalamitsa Beach 🏖 beach card · wind & conditions →

Beautiful sandy bay 10 min from Linaria. Shallow water, good for a morning swim.

14:00

Skyrian Horse reserve

See the island's unique native pony breed — among the smallest horses in the world, nearly extinct. Volunteers run a small sanctuary open to visitors.

17:00

Coffee in Linaria before the ferry

Return to port. Coffee at Almyra before your ferry.

Planning questions

Is 3 days enough for Skyros?

Yes, for a first visit. The plan covers the essentials at a pace that leaves time in the water. With one more day, add a slow beach day with no driving.

Do I need a car on Skyros?

Yes. The beaches and villages are spread out and the bus does not reach the ones that matter; book ahead for July and August.

Where should I stay on Skyros?

The plan sleeps in Skyros Town and Departure, so you are not crossing the island every morning.
