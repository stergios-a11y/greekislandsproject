How the quiz works

You answer a few quick questions — how much beaches matter, your budget, whether nightlife is a feature or a bug, the pace you travel at — and the matcher compares your answers against the scores of all 88 islands. You get your 3 best matches with their numbers, and from there each island’s full guide: day plan, beaches, where to eat.

The scores aren’t computed by an algorithm or scraped from TripAdvisor — they’re one person’s informed judgment, explained openly on the Mission page. And once you’ve picked, the Budget planner shows what the trip will actually cost — updated for 2026.
