Alonnisos vs Skiathos: Which Should You Visit?

Side-by-side comparison of two Greek islands — scores, character, when to visit.

Alonnisos

Sporades · Total 3.8/5

Alonnisos is the least-developed major island in the Aegean — mostly car-free lanes, dense maquis, and a marine park protecting the last monk seal population in Europe.
