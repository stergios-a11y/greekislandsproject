Our verdict

Neighbours with a daily ferry, so this is also a 'both?' question. Santorini (4.8) you know: the caldera, the sunsets, the prices (1.0). Heraklion (4.2) is the anti-Santorini: a working Cretan city with Knossos and a world-class museum, wine villages, and food that locals actually eat, at double the affordability (3.5).

As a straight choice: 2–3 days and a camera — Santorini; a week and an appetite — Heraklion, with a car (5.0). But the smart play is the combination: fly into one, out of the other, with the 2-hour ferry between them. Three nights of caldera spectacle, four of Cretan substance — it fixes both islands' weaknesses, and our Budget page prices the whole route.

Verdict: Santorini for the show, Heraklion for the meal — ideally in that order, connected by ferry.
